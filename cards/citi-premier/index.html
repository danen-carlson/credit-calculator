❓ Frequently asked questions

Is the Citi Premier worth the $95 annual fee?
Easily — if you use the 3x categories. Spend $1,000/month across gas, groceries, and dining and you're earning 36,000 ThankYou Points per year ($360+ in travel value). The $100 annual hotel credit covers most of the fee, leaving just net $5/yr. The signup bonus alone ($600 in travel) covers the fee for 6+ years.
How does the Citi Premier differ from the Citi Strata Premier?
The Citi Strata Premier ($400/yr) is the premium version — it adds airport lounge access (Priority Pass), a $250 annual travel credit, and a higher signup bonus. The regular Citi Premier ($95/yr) is the better value for most people. Only upgrade to Strata if you use airport lounges regularly.
What are Citi ThankYou Points worth?
1¢ each for cash back, gift cards, or statement credits. 1.25¢ each when redeemed for travel through Citi Travel. Transfers to airline partners (JetBlue, Singapore Airlines, Virgin Atlantic, etc.) can yield 1.5–2¢+ per point. The Premier is the key to unlocking transfer partners — without it, ThankYou Points from the Double Cash are worth only 1¢ each.
Can I combine Citi Premier points with Citi Double Cash?
Yes! This is the power move. The Citi Double Cash earns 2x ThankYou Points on everything. With the Premier, those points become transferable to airline/hotel partners at 1¢+ each instead of being locked to 1¢ cash back. The combo is called "CitiDoublePremier" and it's one of the best point-earning setups available.
How does gas station 3x work with EV charging?
The 3x rate applies to both traditional gas stations and EV charging stations. This is rare — most cards only bonus gas, not EV charging. If you drive an EV and charge at public stations, the Premier is one of the best cards for that spend category.
What happens to my points if I downgrade?
If you downgrade the Citi Premier to the Citi Custom Cash or Double Cash, you keep all accumulated ThankYou Points. However, you lose the ability to transfer points to travel partners — they revert to 1¢ cash value only. Transfer your points to partners before downgrading.

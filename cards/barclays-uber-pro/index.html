❓ Frequently asked questions

Is the Barclays Uber Pro Card worth getting?
If you spend $200+/month on Uber rides and Uber Eats, absolutely. The 7% back on Uber is the highest rideshare reward rate available. The Uber One credit alone ($120/yr) makes this a no-brainer for regular users. Even occasional users benefit since there's no annual fee.
How does the 7% cash back on Uber work?
You earn 7% cash back on Uber rides and Uber Eats purchases, credited as Uber Cash to your Uber account. The 5% rate applies to Uber rides specifically — so if you use both Uber Eats and rides, you're earning 7% on the food delivery and 5% on the rides.
What is UberVIP status?
UberVIP gives you priority matching with top-rated drivers in select cities. It's not available everywhere, and the benefit is modest compared to the cash-back rewards. Don't get the card just for UberVIP.
Can I use Uber Cash for anything besides rides and food?
Yes — Uber Cash can be used for rides, Uber Eats orders, and Uber package delivery. It cannot be withdrawn as cash or applied to your credit card balance.
How does this compare to the Amex Gold for food delivery?
Amex Gold gives 4x points on dining worldwide (including Uber Eats), but the Uber Pro gives a flat 7% on Uber Eats specifically. If you eat out at restaurants more than you order delivery, the Gold is better. If Uber Eats is your primary delivery, the Uber Pro is better.
Does the Uber One credit apply automatically?
Yes — if you're an Uber One member, you'll receive a $9.99 monthly statement credit for your membership fee. If you're not a member, you can sign up and the credit covers the cost entirely.

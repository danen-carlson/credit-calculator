❓ Frequently asked questions

Is the Wells Fargo Autograph worth it?
Yes — if you spend on dining, gas, transit, streaming, or phone plans. The 3x rate across 5 categories with no annual fee is one of the best no-fee setups available. The signup bonus ($330 value) is solid for a $0 AF card.
What are Wells Fargo points worth?
Wells Fargo Go Far Rewards points are worth ~1.65¢ each when redeemed for travel through their portal, or 1¢ for cash back. With 11 transfer partners, you can potentially get 1.5–2¢+ per point on airline transfers, though the partner list isn't as deep as Chase or Amex.
How does the Autograph compare to the Chase Freedom Flex?
Autograph gives consistent 3x on 5 categories with no activation needed. Freedom Flex gives 5% on rotating categories (up to $1,500/quarter) which can be more lucrative if you track the calendar, plus 3% on dining and drugstores. Autograph is set-and-forget; Freedom Flex is for people who like to optimize.
Does the Autograph count gas and transit separately?
No — gas stations and transit (rideshare, buses, trains, parking, tolls) are combined into one "gas & transit" category. That means your Uber rides and gas fill-ups both earn 3x.
Is the cell phone protection good?
It covers damage and theft up to $600 per claim ($1,200/year max, $25 deductible). You need to pay your monthly phone bill with the card to be eligible. This alone can save you $10-15/mo if you're paying for phone insurance through your carrier.
Can I downgrade or upgrade later?
You can product-change to other Wells Fargo cards (like the Active Cash for a flat 2% setup). There's no penalty for upgrading/downgrading, but the Autograph is one of the best no-AF cards they offer.

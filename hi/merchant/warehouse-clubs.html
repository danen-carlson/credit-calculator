Best Credit Cards for Costco & Sam's Club (2026)

Warehouse clubs like Costco and Sam's Club are a haul — the average member spends $200–500 per trip. Choosing the right card can earn you 2–5% back on bulk groceries, household goods, gas, and electronics.

Here's the catch most people miss: Costco and Sam's Club code as warehouse clubs, not grocery stores. That means your 3% or 5% grocery bonus cards won't trigger. You need cards that specifically reward warehouse club spending or offer flat-rate 2%+ back.

Also important: Costco only accepts Visa credit cards in-warehouse (all networks on Costco.com). Sam's Club accepts all four major networks. This narrows your options at Costco significantly.

Warehouse Club Strategy: Which Card Where?

Your best card depends on where you shop and what you buy:

  • Costco warehouse: Use the Costco Anywhere Visa (2%) or Citi Double Cash (2%). Grocery bonus cards won't work — Costco codes as warehouse club.
  • Sam's Club: Use the Sam's Club Mastercard (3% at Sam's, 5% gas) or Citi Double Cash (2% flat). More network flexibility than Costco.
  • Costco gas: Use the Costco Visa (4% on gas, first $7K/yr). This is one of the best gas cards available.
  • Costco.com: All major credit cards accepted. Use your highest-rate card — groceries on Amex Blue Cash Preferred (6%), general shopping on Citi Double Cash (2%).

The Costco stacking trick

If you're an Executive Member ($120/yr), you earn 2% on most Costco purchases (up to $1,000/year reward). Stack that with the Costco Visa's 2% back and you're earning 4% total on every Costco purchase — without even trying. The membership fee pays for itself at $500+/month in Costco spending.

Best Credit Cards for Online Shopping (2026)

Online shopping accounts for 20–30% of most households' discretionary spending. From Amazon Prime deliveries to Target runs to specialty stores, choosing the right card can earn you an extra $100–300/year in cash back on purchases you'd make anyway.

The key insight: not all online purchases code the same way. Amazon codes as a book store or general merchandise. Target.com codes as online retail. Subscription services like Netflix and Spotify code as streaming. Each category has different bonus structures.

The best strategy depends on where you shop most. Heavy Amazon users want a dedicated Amazon card (5%). Diverse online shoppers should focus on flat-rate cards (2%) or online shopping bonus cards (3%). And the Chase Freedom Flex's rotating 5% category often includes Amazon in Q4.

The 3-Card Online Shopping Strategy

Most online shoppers do best with three cards:

  1. Amazon Prime Visa (5% at Amazon) — For all Amazon and Whole Foods purchases. Keep this in your Amazon wallet as default.
  2. Amex Blue Cash Everyday (3% online retail) — For Target.com, Walmart.com, and other non-Amazon online shopping. The $6K/yr cap covers $500/month of online spending.
  3. Citi Double Cash (2% flat) — For everything else. This is your catch-all for sites that don't earn bonuses on either card above.

This setup earns 2–5% on virtually 100% of your online spending, with no annual fees on any of them.

What about the Chase Freedom Flex rotating 5%?

Add the Chase Freedom Flex as a fourth card when Q4 rolls around — Amazon is almost always in the 5% rotating category October through December. That's peak holiday shopping at 5% with no effort.

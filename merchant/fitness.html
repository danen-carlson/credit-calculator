How Fitness Purchases Code

Understanding how your fitness spending codes is the key to picking the right card:

  • Gym memberships (Planet Fitness, LA Fitness, Equinox): Usually code as MCC 7997 (Health Clubs) or fall under general merchandise. The US Bank Cash+ specifically includes "gym memberships" as a 5% category.
  • Fitness apps (Peloton, Apple Fitness+, Nike Training Club): Usually code as streaming or online shopping. Cards with streaming bonuses (Amex Blue Cash Preferred at 6%, Freedom Flex at 3%) can earn extra.
  • Peloton equipment: Codes as online/retail purchase, not fitness. Use a card with an online shopping bonus.
  • Personal training: Varies — some code as health clubs, others as professional services. Check your statement.
  • Sports equipment (dumbbells, bands, bench): Usually codes as sporting goods or general merchandise.

Best Strategy: Stack Two Cards

Since no single card covers all fitness categories at a bonus rate, use a two-card strategy:

  1. US Bank Cash+ — Set "gym memberships" as a 5% category for in-person gym dues
  2. BofA Customized Cash Rewards — Set "online shopping" as your 3% category for fitness apps and digital subscriptions

This combo earns 3-5% on nearly all fitness spending with no annual fees.

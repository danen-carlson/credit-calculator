Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data. Last updated: May 15, 2026.

Introduction

CreditStud.io ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website creditstud.io and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our site, use our calculators, or subscribe to our newsletter.

By using CreditStud.io, you agree to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide Directly

  • Email address — When you subscribe to our newsletter via Beehiiv, we collect your email address to send you updates and financial tips.
  • Contact form submissions — If you contact us via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content.

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data — We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect usage data such as pages visited, time on site, referral source, device type, browser, and general location (country/city level). GA4 collects this data using cookies and similar technologies.
  • Calculator state — Our financial calculators use your browser's localStorage to save your inputs (e.g., balance, APR, term length) so your work is preserved between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Standard server logs — Our hosting provider may log IP addresses, request timestamps, and browser user-agent strings as part of routine server operation.

3. Information from Third Parties

  • Affiliate networks — When you click an affiliate link (to apply for a credit card or financial product), the affiliate network (e.g., Commission Junction, Impact, FlexOffers) may place its own cookies or tracking parameters to attribute any subsequent application or approval. These trackers are governed by the respective network's privacy policy, not ours.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — Required for basic site functionality (e.g., language preference, session continuity). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — Set by Google Analytics 4 to measure traffic, engagement, and usage patterns. These cookies collect anonymized data and help us improve the site. You can opt out of GA4 tracking using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Affiliate tracking cookies — Placed by third-party affiliate networks (CJ, Impact, FlexOffers) when you click an "Apply Now" link. These cookies track whether your application results in a commission for CreditStud.io. They do not collect personal information beyond what the affiliate network needs for attribution.

We do not use retargeting or advertising cookies from ad networks (Google Ads, Meta, etc.).

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain our calculators and website
  • Send newsletter emails if you have subscribed (via Beehiiv)
  • Analyze site usage and improve content and functionality
  • Respond to your inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Sharing

We share limited data with the following third parties:

  • Beehiiv — Your email address is shared with Beehiiv, our newsletter platform, solely for the purpose of sending you our newsletter. Beehiiv's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any newsletter email.
  • Affiliate networks (CJ, Impact, FlexOffers) — When you click an affiliate link, click and conversion data is shared with the relevant affiliate network for commission tracking. We do not share your email, name, or other personal data with affiliate networks.
  • Google Analytics — Anonymized usage data is processed by Google Analytics. Google may use this data per its own Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

Data Storage and Security

  • Calculator inputs are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Email addresses are stored by Beehiiv on secure, encrypted servers.
  • We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any data we process.
  • No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

  • Newsletter subscriptions — Retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Contact form submissions — Retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Analytics data — Retained by Google Analytics for 14 months, then automatically aggregated or deleted per GA4 settings.
  • Calculator state — Stored in localStorage until you clear your browser data.

Your Rights

Opt-Out Rights

  • Newsletter — Unsubscribe via the link in any email, or contact privacy@creditstud.io.
  • Analytics — Use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Affiliate tracking — Clear your browser cookies or use a private/incognito window.
  • Calculator state — Clear your browser's localStorage via browser settings.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Delete their personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)
  • Non-discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@creditstud.io. We will respond to verified requests within 45 days.

We do not sell personal information. This has been our policy since launch and will not change.

Affiliate Disclosure

In accordance with FTC guidelines (16 CFR Part 255), we disclose that CreditStud.io may receive compensation when you click on links to credit card products and submit an application. This compensation may impact how and where products appear on the site. Our calculator rankings are based solely on the math — total cost or reward value to you — and are never influenced by compensation. For full details, see our Affiliate Disclosure.

Children's Privacy

CreditStud.io is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 18, please contact us at privacy@creditstud.io and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
